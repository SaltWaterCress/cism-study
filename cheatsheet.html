Risk formulas

SLE = Asset value (AV) × Exposure factor (EF)
ALE = SLE × ARO (annual rate of occurrence)
Control value = ALE before − ALE after − annual control cost

Example: a $500k asset with 40% exposure → SLE $200k. Expected once every 4 years (ARO 0.25) → ALE $50k/yr. A control costing more than the risk it removes isn't justified.

Recovery objectives

TermPlain meaning
RTOHow fast the process must be back up
RPOHow much data (time) you can afford to lose → drives backup frequency
MTD / MTPDLongest outage before the damage is unacceptable. RTO ≤ MTD
SDOMinimum service level needed while in recovery mode

Lower RTO and RPO means higher cost. Both come from the BIA, which business process owners provide input to.

Incident response phases

Prepare→Identify / detect→Contain→Eradicate→Recover→Lessons learned

Event = anything observable. Incident = an event that harms or threatens the organisation. Preserve evidence and chain of custody before cleaning up.
