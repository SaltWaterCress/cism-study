    A simple study plan

    About 6–8 weeks at 1 hour a day. Adjust to your exam date.

    WeekFocus
    1Domain 1 study guide + flashcards. Read the mindset rules daily.
    2Domain 2. Learn the risk formulas and appetite / tolerance / capacity.
    3–4Domain 3 (the biggest). Spend extra time on architecture, controls and vendors.
    5Domain 4. BIA, RTO/RPO, recovery sites, test types, IR phases.
    6Domain drills on weak areas. Review every wrong answer's explanation.
    7–8Full mock exams under time pressure. Aim for 75%+ before booking.
